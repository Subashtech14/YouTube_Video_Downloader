Download videos from any site.

Pick a site, paste a link, choose a quality. Downloads run in parallel pieces over many connections.

WhatsApp videos can't be downloaded from a link. They're end-to-end encrypted inside your chats and have no public URL. To save one:
  1. Open the chat and tap the video.
  2. On iPhone tap Share, then Save. On Android tap the three-dot menu, then Save.
  3. On WhatsApp Desktop or Web, hover the video and choose Download.
WhatsApp Channels videos work the same way: save them from the app.

Saving WhatsApp Statuses? Use the AnySave Android app. Its WhatsApp tab has a Status saver that shows the statuses you've viewed and saves them to your Gallery. A computer can't see your phone's statuses.

Tip: paste a link and it loads automatically. Press Enter to fetch.
